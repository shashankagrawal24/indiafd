Deposit Interest Rates

TMB has been consistently offering higher rates of interest for the past several years for the benefit of its depositors since its cost of operations is low compared to other banks. The rates are split for different segments to offer the highest rate of interest possible. Apart from the Regular Domestic Deposits, we have special rates for High Value Deposits as well as even higher interest rates for Senior Citizens of India.

As per the section 206AA of the Income Tax Act, quoting of Permanent Account Number (PAN) is mandatory, if the interest on term deposits for the financial year exceeds Rs. 50,000.00 for ordinary citizens and Rs. 1,00,000.00 for senior citizens. If PAN is not furnished, then tax has to be deducted at higher rate of 20%. Quoting of PAN in the declaration form 121 is mandatory for non-deduction of tax at source. If PAN is not quoted in the form 121, tax will be deducted at a higher rate of 20%.

Therefore, the customers are requested to furnish their PAN to the Bank and those customers who are eligible to submit Form 121 are requested to submit Form No. 121 with their Permanent Account Number (PAN) along with the photocopy of the PAN Card immediately.
