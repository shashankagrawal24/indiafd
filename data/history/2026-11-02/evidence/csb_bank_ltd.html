Domestic Recurring Deposits are accepted for a minimum period of 6 months & thereafter in multiples of one month.

In the case of premature closure of Fixed Deposits (FD), the applicable interest rate would be the card rate prevalent on the date of the deposit for the period the deposit remained in the bank or the contracted rate, whichever is lower less applicable penalty (presently 1% penalty for premature closure of Domestic - FD).

In case of pre-closure of Recurring Deposit (RD), the applicable interest rate would be the card rate prevalent on the date of the deposit for the completed months of the RD or the contracted rate, whichever is lower less applicable penalty (presently 1% penalty for premature closure of Domestic-RD). No interest will be paid if the term deposit is closed prior to completion of minimum tenor as prescribed by RBI from time to time.


Term Deposit booked under ‘Tax Savings product/scheme’ cannot be withdrawn till the completion of the lock-in period (presently 5 years) applicable for that deposit. Also Term Deposits booked under ‘Non-callable product/scheme’ cannot be withdrawn till the maturity of that deposit.


Additional interest rates given to staffs for ‘Resident Term Deposit (TD) in INR’ has been increased to 0.50% p.a. (earlier 0.15% p.a.). The additional interest rate on ‘Resident SB accounts in INR’ remains unchanged at 0.50% p.a. over and above the regular SB account interest rate.

Additional interest rates given to Senior Citizen customer for ‘Resident Term Deposit in INR’ has been increased to 0.25% p.a. (from 0.15% p.a)
