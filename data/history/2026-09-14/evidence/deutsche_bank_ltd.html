Savings and Deposit Interest Rates

Savings Interest Rate

Effective August 01, 2025,

  • Rate of Interest for Savings Accounts has been revised to 2.50% per annum.
  • Rate of Interest for RFC Savings Accounts / All other FCY Savings Account has been revised to 0.01% per annum 

Note

  • In line with RBI guidelines with effect from 1st April 2010, Savings Bank interest is calculated on daily balances maintained in your account.
  • While calculating Savings Bank interest to be paid on a daily basis and the Bank will consider 365 days for both leap and non-leap years. 
  • The calculation logic for calculating Savings Bank interest will be Daily Balance X Rate of Interest X (Number of days) / 365 X 100.
  • Interest on savings bank accounts, including those frozen by the enforcement authorities, shall be credited on regular basis irrespective of the operational status of the account.
  • Savings Bank interest will be paid at quarterly intervals i.e. March, June, September and December.
  • This rate is subject to change as per the discretion of the bank.
