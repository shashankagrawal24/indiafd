Got questions? We've got answers.

Karnataka Bank offers a wide range of FD options tailored to different customer profiles, including tax-saving and high-return schemes, making it a go-to choice for the best fixed deposit rates.

You can start a fixed deposit at Karnataka Bank with as little as ₹100. This low entry point makes it accessible for investors. The tenure ranges from 7 days to 120 months, and the FD interest rate varies based on the amount and duration. Whether you're looking for short-term savings or long-term wealth creation, Karnataka Bank offers some of the best FD rates in the industry with flexible investment options.

Yes, NRIs can invest in fixed deposits through Karnataka Bank’s NRE, NRO, and FCNR deposit schemes. These accounts offer competitive NRI FD interest rates and benefits like tax-free interest on NRE deposits and full repatriation of funds. NRIs can also open joint accounts with other NRIs or resident relatives. With flexible tenures and secure returns, Karnataka Bank is a trusted choice for NRIs seeking the best fixed deposit interest rates in India.

Callable FDs allow premature withdrawal before maturity, usually with a penalty. Non-callable FDs, on the other hand, do not permit early withdrawal, but they offer higher interest rates in return. Karnataka Bank provides both options, with non-callable FDs offering enhanced returns for select tenures. Choosing between callable and non-callable depends on your liquidity needs and investment horizon. For higher returns, non-callable FDs are among the best fixed deposit options.

The bank has statutory obligation to deduct tax at source if the total interest paid/payable on all term deposits held by a person exceeds the amount specified under the Income Tax Act. The Bank will issue a Tax deduction certificate (TDS Certificate) for the amount of tax deducted. The depositor, if entitled to exemption from TDS can submit declaration in the prescribed format (15G/15H) at the beginning of every financial year.

A Term Deposit and Fixed Deposit are often used interchangeably, but they can differ slightly. A Fixed Deposit typically refers to a lump-sum investment for a fixed tenure, while Term Deposit is a broader term that includes both fixed and recurring deposits . Both offer fixed interest rates, low risk, and are ideal for safe, short- to medium-term savings.

The current FD interest rate at Karnataka Bank varies by tenure. Kindly refer the above interest rates. These fixed deposit interest rates ensure flexibility for short‑term and long‑term savings depending on customer needs.

Yes, Karnataka Bank offers higher rate of Interest to Resident Indian Senior Citizens who have completed the age of 60 years on their Term deposits as specified by Bank from time to time.

The FD interest rate is mainly influenced by the deposit tenure, deposit amount, and customer category such as regular or senior citizen. Market conditions and bank policies also impact fixed deposit interest rates. Generally, longer tenures may offer better returns.

Term Deposit interest rates are decided by bank within the guidelines issued by Reserved Bank of India from time to time. The rate of interest on Term deposit/schedule of interest rates will be prominently displayed in the branch premises. Changes, if any, with regards to the deposit schemes and other related services will be communicated upfront by promptly displaying at branch premise and on website.
