Interest Rates On Loans & Advances

In terms of guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India regarding implementation of External Benchmark Based Lending rates, Bank has introduced Repo Linked Lending Rates for all floating rate loans to Personal, Retail and Micro/ Small enterprises w.e.f. 1st October,2019 and for all floating rate loans to Medium enterprises w.e.f. 1st April, 2020.

 

For loans to Personal, Retail and Micro/ Small/ Medium enterprises, applicable Repo Rate is 5.25% w.e.f. February 01, 2026

The actual lending rate will be Repo Rate plus applicable spread for the said loan.

Effective from 01.04.2016, all rupee loans sanctioned/ disbursed and credit limits renewed will be priced with reference to the MCLR. MCLR is subject to monthly review and the rates given below will be effective till next review. Tenor-based MCLR effective from 1st October, 2026 is as under:

Information on MCLR

The MCLR is computed by taking into account –

  • Marginal cost of funds
  • Negative Carry on Cash Reserve Ratio
  • Operating costs and
  • Tenor Premium

MCLR is the minimum lending rate. Bank's final lending rates for various types of loans and advances are arrived at by adding the spread to the MCLR based on rating or tenor corresponding to the residual period to next interest reset of the loan.

MCLR prevailing on the day, the loan is disbursed will be applicable till the next reset date, irrespective of the changes in the benchmark during the interim. The reset dates are linked to the MCLR review date and reset frequency.

Borrowers whose accounts are linked to BPLR/ Base Rate can approach the Branch Manager for conversion of their loan accounts to MCLR linked interest rates, at mutually acceptable terms.

The Base Rate of the Bank is 11.90 % p.a.  w.e.f 3rd September, 2024.

Benchmark Prime Lending Rate(BPLR) of the Bank is 21% p. a. w.e.f   2nd November, 2011
